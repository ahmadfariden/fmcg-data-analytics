# 25. Promotion Analysis
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

Promotion Analysis evaluates the effectiveness of promotional activities in driving sales growth, revenue generation, profitability, and inventory movement.

The objective is to determine:

- Whether promotions generate incremental sales
- Which promotions deliver the highest return
- Which products benefit most from promotions
- Whether promotions improve inventory efficiency
- Which promotions should be expanded, optimized, or discontinued

---

# Promotion Revenue Analysis

## Business Question

How much revenue is generated during promotional activities?

---

## KPI

```text
Promo Revenue
```

### Formula

```text
Revenue where Promo_Flag = 1
```

---

## Findings

Promotional activities contribute a significant portion of total sales revenue.

However, performance varies substantially across products and categories.

---

## Business Impact

Promotions can:

- Increase Revenue
- Increase Volume
- Improve Market Penetration

But may also reduce profitability if discounts are excessive.

---

# Promotion Contribution Analysis

## Business Question

How dependent is revenue on promotions?

---

## KPI

```text
Promotion Contribution %
```

### Formula

```text
Promo Revenue
/
Total Revenue
```

---

## Findings

Some categories rely heavily on promotions to generate sales.

Others perform strongly even without promotional support.

---

## Business Impact

High dependency on promotions may indicate:

- Pricing sensitivity
- Competitive pressure
- Weak brand loyalty

---

# Sales Uplift Analysis

## Business Question

Do promotions generate incremental sales?

---

## KPI

```text
Sales Uplift %
```

### Formula

```text
(Promo Sales - Non-Promo Sales)
/
Non-Promo Sales
```

---

## Findings

Not all promotions create meaningful sales uplift.

Several promotions simply shift demand timing without increasing overall sales.

---

## Business Impact

Sales uplift analysis helps distinguish:

- Effective promotions
- Ineffective promotions
- Cannibalization effects

---

# Promotion ROI Analysis

## Business Question

Which promotions generate the highest return?

---

## KPI

```text
Promotion ROI
```

### Formula

```text
Incremental Gross Profit
/
Promotion Cost
```

---

## Findings

Certain promotions generate strong revenue growth but weak profitability.

Others create moderate sales increases with strong ROI.

---

## Business Impact

ROI analysis prevents overinvestment in low-performing promotions.

# Promotion Risk Assessment

## Risk 1

### Margin Erosion Risk

Excessive discounting reduces profitability.

---

## Risk 2

### Revenue Dependency Risk

Sales become overly dependent on promotions.

---

## Risk 3

### Promotion Cannibalization

Promotions shift demand timing rather than creating incremental demand.

---

## Risk 4

### Inventory Risk

Poorly planned promotions may create stock shortages.

---

# Strategic Insights

## Insight 1

Promotions significantly influence sales performance, but effectiveness varies widely.

## Insight 2

Revenue growth does not always translate into profitable promotions.

---

## Insight 3

---

## Insight 4

## Insight 5

# Business Recommendations

## Promotion Optimization

- Prioritize promotions with proven ROI performance.
- Focus investment on high-response products.

---

## Inventory Management

- Use promotions to reduce slow-moving inventory.
- Avoid unnecessary discounts on strong-performing products.

---

## Commercial Planning

- Integrate promotion performance reviews into monthly business reviews.
- Monitor uplift, margin, and ROI simultaneously.

---

## Profitability Management

- Balance sales growth objectives with margin protection.
- Reduce spending on low-performing campaigns.

---

## Promotion Governance

- Establish promotion scorecards.
- Track performance at campaign, product, and branch levels.
