# 24. Product Performance Analysis
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

Product Performance Analysis evaluates the commercial performance, profitability, demand behavior, and inventory efficiency of products across the FMCG portfolio.

The objective is to identify:

- Top-performing products
- Underperforming products
- Revenue drivers
- Profitability opportunities
- Inventory optimization opportunities
- Product portfolio risks

---

# Revenue Analysis

## Business Question

Which products generate the highest revenue?

---

## KPI

```text
Revenue
```

### Formula

```text
SUM(Net Revenue)
```

---

## Findings

Revenue contribution is highly concentrated among a relatively small group of products.

This pattern follows the typical FMCG Pareto distribution.

---

## Business Impact

Top products become critical drivers of:

- Revenue Growth
- Market Share
- Inventory Planning

# Business Recommendations

## Revenue Growth

- Increase focus on high-performing products.
- Expand market penetration for growth products.

---

## Profitability Improvement

- Prioritize products with strong margin performance.
- Review low-margin products for optimization opportunities.

---

## Inventory Optimization

- Allocate inventory based on demand behavior.
- Improve replenishment planning for critical products.

---

## Portfolio Rationalization

- Review low-performing SKUs regularly.
- Reduce inventory investment in underperforming products.

---

## Product Governance

- Establish monthly product performance reviews.
- Monitor revenue, growth, margin, and inventory KPIs continuously.
