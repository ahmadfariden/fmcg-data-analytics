# 20. Sales Performance Analysis
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

The Sales Performance Analysis evaluates revenue generation, profitability, growth trends, target achievement, and sales contribution across branches, products, distributors, and customer segments.

The objective is to identify:

- Revenue drivers
- Growth opportunities
- Underperforming areas
- Profitability trends
- Commercial risks
- Strategic business opportunities

---

```text

        ↓

```

# Revenue Analysis

## Business Question

How much revenue was generated and where did it come from?

---

## Metrics

```text
Revenue
Gross Revenue
Net Revenue
Revenue Contribution %
```

---

## Analysis Dimensions

- Time
- Region
- Branch
- Product
- Brand
- Distributor
- Customer Segment
- Sales Channel

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Concentration

Revenue is primarily generated by a relatively small group of branches and products.

This follows a typical Pareto distribution pattern commonly found in FMCG businesses.

---

### Revenue Distribution

Revenue contribution varies significantly across:

- Regions
- Branches
- Product Categories
- Distributors

# Business Recommendations

## Revenue Growth

- Replicate successful branch strategies across similar markets.
- Expand support for high-growth branches.

---

## Product Portfolio

- Prioritize investment in high-performing products.
- Review low-performing products for rationalization opportunities.

---

## Distributor Management

- Reduce dependency on a small group of distributors.
- Strengthen distributor performance monitoring.

---

## Branch Improvement

- Implement recovery plans for underperforming branches.
- Reallocate commercial resources to high-potential markets.

---

## Profitability Optimization

- Focus on products and customer segments with strong margin performance.
- Balance revenue growth with profitability objectives.
