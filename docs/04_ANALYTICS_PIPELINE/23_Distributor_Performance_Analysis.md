# 23. Distributor Performance Analysis
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

Distributor Performance Analysis evaluates the effectiveness, service quality, revenue contribution, and operational reliability of distributors across the FMCG distribution network.

The objective is to identify:

- Best-performing distributors
- At-risk distributors
- Service-level issues
- Revenue dependency risks
- Distribution efficiency opportunities
- Supply chain improvement initiatives

# OTIF Performance Analysis

## Business Question

Are distributors delivering products on time and in full?

---

## KPI

```text
OTIF %
```

### Formula

```text
On-Time-In-Full Deliveries
/
Total Deliveries
```

---

## Performance Categories

| OTIF % | Classification |
|---------|---------------|
| ≥ 98% | Excellent |
| 95% - 97.99% | Good |
| 90% - 94.99% | Improvement Needed |
| < 90% | Critical Risk |

---

## Findings

OTIF performance varies significantly across distributors.

Several high-revenue distributors show declining service levels.

---

## Business Impact

Poor OTIF performance can cause:

- Stockouts
- Lost Sales
- Service Failures
- Customer Complaints

# Business Recommendations

## Strategic Partnership Management

- Strengthen relationships with top-performing distributors.
- Develop joint business planning initiatives.

---

## Service Improvement

- Monitor OTIF and lead time KPIs monthly.
- Establish distributor performance scorecards.

---

## Risk Mitigation

- Reduce dependency on a small number of distributors.
- Diversify distribution coverage where necessary.

---

## Capacity Optimization

---

## Performance Recovery

- Monitor improvement progress through quarterly reviews.
