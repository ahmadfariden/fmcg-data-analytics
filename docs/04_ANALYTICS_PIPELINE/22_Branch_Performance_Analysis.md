# 22. Branch Performance Analysis
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

Branch Performance Analysis evaluates branch-level business performance by measuring revenue contribution, profitability, target achievement, growth trends, and operational effectiveness.

The objective is to identify:

- Top-performing branches
- Underperforming branches
- Growth opportunities
- Regional performance differences
- Resource allocation priorities
- Commercial improvement opportunities

---

```text
Branch Revenue Analysis

```

# Revenue Analysis

## Business Question

Which branches generate the highest revenue?

---

## KPI

```text
Revenue
```

### Formula

```text
SUM(Net Revenue)
```

---

## Findings

Revenue contribution is not evenly distributed across branches.

A relatively small number of branches generate a significant portion of total company revenue.

---

High-performing branches become key drivers of:

- Revenue Growth

- Profitability

# Target Achievement Analysis

## Business Question

Which branches achieve their sales targets?

---

## KPI

```text
Achievement %
```

### Formula

```text
Actual Revenue
/
Revenue Target
```

---

## Achievement Categories

| Achievement % | Classification |
|---------------|---------------|
| ≥ 120% | Exceptional |
| 100% - 119% | On Target |
| 90% - 99% | Slightly Below Target |
| < 90% | Underperforming |

---

## Findings

Several branches consistently exceed targets.

Others struggle despite operating in high-potential markets.

---

## Business Impact

Low achievement may indicate:

- Market issues
- Execution issues
- Resource constraints
- Target planning problems

---

# Growth Analysis

## Business Question

Which branches are growing and which are declining?

---

## KPI

```text
Growth %
```

### Formula

```text
(Current Revenue - Previous Revenue)
/
Previous Revenue
```

---

## Findings

Growth performance varies significantly across branches.

# Branch Risk Assessment

## Risk 1

### Revenue Concentration Risk

Heavy dependence on a limited number of branches.

---

## Risk 2

### Growth Risk

Declining performance in key markets.

---

## Risk 3

### Target Achievement Risk

Persistent underachievement against sales plans.

---

## Risk 4

### Market Opportunity Risk

Failure to capitalize on high-potential markets.

---

# Strategic Insights

## Insight 1

A small number of branches contribute a disproportionate share of company revenue.

---

## Insight 2

Several branches consistently outperform both targets and growth expectations.

---

## Insight 3

Underperforming branches are concentrated in specific regions and market segments.

---

## Insight 4

Market potential and actual performance are not always aligned.

---

## Insight 5

Branch profitability varies significantly even among branches with similar revenue levels.

---

# Business Recommendations

## Revenue Growth

- Prioritize investment in top-performing branches.
- Expand successful sales strategies into similar markets.

---

## Performance Recovery

- Implement recovery plans for low-performing branches.
- Monitor achievement trends monthly.

---

## Resource Allocation

- Allocate inventory and sales resources based on performance and market potential.

---

## Market Expansion

- Focus growth initiatives on high-potential branches with low achievement.

---

## Performance Governance

- Establish branch performance scorecards.
- Conduct monthly branch performance reviews.

---

# Branch Performance Outcome

The Branch Performance Analysis provides a comprehensive view of:

- Revenue Contribution
- Target Achievement
- Growth Trends
- Profitability
- Regional Performance
- Market Opportunity
- Operational Effectiveness

The analysis enables management to optimize branch operations, improve resource allocation, strengthen commercial execution, and drive sustainable revenue growth across the FMCG distribution network.
