# 26. Operational KPI Calculation
## FMCG Enterprise Analytics Dashboard

---

# Purpose

Operational KPI Calculation defines the core business metrics used to monitor commercial performance, supply chain efficiency, inventory health, distributor effectiveness, and overall business operations.

These KPIs serve as the foundation for:

- Executive Reporting
- Branch Monitoring
- Distributor Evaluation
- Inventory Optimization
- Commercial Decision Making
- Strategic Planning

---

# KPI Framework

```text
Commercial KPI
        ↓
Inventory KPI
        ↓
Distributor KPI
        ↓
Operational KPI
        ↓
Product KPI
        ↓
Executive KPI
```

# Executive Summary KPIs

---

## Revenue

### Definition

Total net sales generated by the company.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(Net_Revenue)
```

### Business Purpose

Measure total business performance.

---

## Gross Profit

### Definition

Revenue remaining after deducting product costs.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(Gross_Profit)
```

### Business Purpose

Measure profitability.

---

## Margin %

### Definition

Percentage of profit generated from sales.

### Formula

```sql
SUM(Gross_Profit)
/
SUM(Net_Revenue)
```

### Business Purpose

Measure profit efficiency.

---

## Achievement %

### Definition

Actual revenue versus planned target.

### Formula

```sql
Revenue
/
Revenue Target
```

### Business Purpose

Measure business target attainment.

---

## Growth %

### Definition

Revenue growth compared to previous period.

### Formula

```sql
(Current Revenue - Previous Revenue)
/
Previous Revenue
```

### Business Purpose

Measure business expansion.

# KPI Calculation Outcome

The KPI framework standardizes performance measurement across:

- Executive Management
- Commercial Teams
- Supply Chain Teams
- Inventory Management
- Distributor Management
- Category Management

These KPIs provide a consistent measurement system that supports data-driven decision-making across the entire FMCG distribution business.
